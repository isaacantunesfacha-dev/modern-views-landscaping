Terms of service

Starting prices published on this website reflect typical residential scopes in Greater Houston and are confirmed after an on-site grade, access and utility check.

3D plans and HOA submittals

Scaled 3D landscape plans and HOA architectural review packets are prepared for the specific lot surveyed. Construction begins once written HOA approval and utility line markings (Texas 811) are complete.

Written proposals

All drainage, irrigation, concrete, stone and carpentry scopes are governed by the signed written project agreement issued by Modern Views Landscaping LLC.
