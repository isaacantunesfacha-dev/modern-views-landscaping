Standing water along Houston slabs and fences

New sod rots quickly on saturated Houston gumbo clay when rainwater has no exit route. We shoot laser elevations across the side yard and install catch basins and gravel-packed French drains discharging to the street curb.

3D landscape plans for Houston HOA committees

Homeowners in Summerwood, Fall Creek, Kingwood, The Woodlands and Bridgeland receive scaled 3D architectural views, lot line setbacks, pergola roof heights and plant schedules formatted for neighborhood review boards.

Compacted stone bases for Houston backyard patios

We excavate organic topsoil down to stable subgrade, compact six inches of crushed limestone aggregate in lifts, tie steel rebar grid and pitch every hardscape slab two percent away from the house foundation.

Zoned drip irrigation and cedar pergola footings

Four build stages from lot survey to cedar pergola

Every backyard project runs in structural order so drainage, stone slabs and timber roofs stay level.

  1. Scaled 3D plan and HOA review

    We shoot elevations across your yard, mark easements and build a 3D architectural model showing sun shade, paver joints and plant massing before HOA submission.

  2. Clay trenching and French drains

    Standing water gets intercepted at the source with filter-wrapped washed gravel trenches, rigid four-inch perforated pipe and flush stainless catch basins.

  3. Compacted stone base and rebar slab

    We compact six inches of crushed limestone road base, tie steel rebar grid and concrete pier footings, and set honed Texas limestone at a two-percent drainage slope.

  4. Steel hardware and cedar carpentry

    Outdoor structures pair powder-coated structural steel columns and stainless brackets with kiln-dried Western Red Cedar louvers and zoned drip irrigation.
